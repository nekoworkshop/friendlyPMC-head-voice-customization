RELEASE NOTES (3.6.5):
- updated get back and cover me commands where in addition to following at a distance, "get back" also makes followers no longer prioritize player position and to rush to his aid. "Cover Me" resets this
- attempt to fix follower still getting stuck in healing animation under certain circumnstances
- altered "get back", "cover me" and "hold position" voices to always say the same thing instead of going through different phrases in order to bring them more closer to what the commands do relative to the followers
- altered how enemy is added to followers as the current method would make followers be aware of any enemy position as soon as that enemy made the player his goal target.

Notes: The way an enemy was added to the followers was by observing who the current target is for any bot. This created situations where your followers would instantly pick up the enemy position and even run to him. This is changed now, but it might feel different as you can now have situations where an enemy is shooting at you from a distance, not hitting you, which in turn makes your followers not react. "Contact" command makes them become aware of the situation.
