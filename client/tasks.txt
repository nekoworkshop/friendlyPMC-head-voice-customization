RELEASE NOTES (3.7):
- !! all server configuration options has been moved into the Configuration Manager,you can now update them through that and they take effect at raid start

- updated Come Here, That Direction and Stop gesture commands where you can now use them to move bots one by one where you want them. 
- updated some Phrase commands to be able to be used on invidual followers by looking at the them. Be careful, bots too close in your view will be picked up and thus the command will not go for all. Being in bushes can interfere with this or not seeing the bot clearly.
- altered "get back", "cover me", "hold position" and "go go go" voices to always say the same thing instead of going through different phrases in order to bring them more closer to what the commands do relative to the followers

- added "On Your Own" command. This will tell bots to stay at a distance (similar to "Get Back"), switch to their default tactic and no longer prioritize your position and safety during fights. "Cover Me" resets this
- added field to provide custom nickname for each follower in the Configuration Manager
- added fields to provide custom uniform (top and bottom) for each follower in the Configuration Manager
- added "Raid End Messages" in the config manager to enable/disabled messages followers may send at the end of the raid (return loot messages are excluded)

- fixed issue with long spawn delay that was happening for some people
- attempt to fix follower still getting stuck in healing animation under certain circumnstances (still not 100% of the cause or how to replicate)
- altered how enemy is added to followers as the current method would make followers be aware of any enemy position as soon as that enemy made the player his goal target.

Notes: 
The way an enemy was added to the followers was by observing who the current target is for any bot. This created situations where your followers would instantly pick up the enemy position and even run to him. This is changed now, but it might feel different as you can now have situations where an enemy is shooting at you from a distance, not hitting you, which in turn makes your followers not react. "Contact" command makes them become aware of the situation. 

How to succesfully use the gesture commands in order to move your followers one by one:
Always start with the Stop gesture and look at a bot, maxium 25m distance, and do the Come To Me gesture. The bot will come near the position your are and then switch back to hold. Repeat as necessary.
Using That Direction works at a maxium 15m and it will affect the closest bot. Best to do it with your back turned to him. Once the bot reaches the point it will switch back to hold. Repeat as necessary.

You can now use the following commands while looking at a bot, at a maximum 35 meters distance, to affect only him : Hold Position, Go Go Go, Get Back, Cover Me, On Your Own, Stop and Follow Me.


Known Issues:
- your followers can have an eagle eye observation or they can be as unaware as a drunken person.
- your followers can end up teleporting. It's what the game does if it detects no viable path from the bot's position to the desired spot. Most notably during "come to me" requests.
- avoid interrupting your both while healing
- SAIN does x, y,z - can't the followers do the same? NO. Sain is not designed to have bots work with the player as a team at all. SAIN is not designed to take into account a cross map moving of a bot making decisions on what to ignore, what to engage. SAIN is focused on combat engagement and affects bot's senses in a way that would make your follower pick up targets left and right. SAIN can put the bot in cover holding when it detects enemy out of sight. That is not what you want your followers to do as you move around the map. So while SAIN has some affect on the followers, its minimal - think of it as dealing with 2 different brains here.
