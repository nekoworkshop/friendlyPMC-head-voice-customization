RELEASE NOTES (3.8):
- removed "Player Equipment" as option for equipment for bots. When clonning player equipment people have reported to have delays in spawn. Because you can simply save your current equipment as a preset loadout - this is removed and you can use that instead. Also I encourge using custom presets that you personally made for bots because people tend to put the meds in the secure container of their own equipment, not realizing that will get deleted and ammo will be put instead for the bots to use. It will also not be there in the end when followers use the equipment you give them instead of clonning it. 
- added a new tactic - Guard who prioritizes to be between you and the enemy
- some improvements on the cover search logic in an effort to reduce the situations where bots end up running in a wall
- added checks to attempt to unstuck followers who look like they are running in place
- made push order act like the old way where follower goes up in the face to the enemy and shoots him
- made the look direction commands (In Front, On Six, etc...) have bots turn to the said direction relative to the direction the player is looking. This works in combat also as long as they do not have an enemy already in sight
