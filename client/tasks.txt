3.3.0 - changes:
- Added "Attention" command to help with stucked bots
- Added "followerstome" console command to teleport bots to player
- Improvements on enemy approach logic
- Bots will get their ammo refill on becoming a follower
- sameSideHostile will no longer turn the followers you spawned with hostile towards you
- Added new command under team "Check In" which will ping the location of your followers
- Added test for spawning with the Goons Squad, optionally just with one of them
- Commands and Gestures the goons listen to:
- - Regroup, Need Help (in combat) - will try to get near player
- - That Direction - will try to approach enemy (in combat) or will try to go in front of player (out of combat)
- - Come Here - will try to go near player (out of combat)

Gameplay of the Goons:
Knight follows the player closely, BirdEye follows the player at a distance and BigPipe follows BirdEye closely
Knight and BigPipe have a close-in combat while BirdEye tries to find spots to snipe from
